◊ What is a sigil ◊

A compressed intention

A sigil is an old technology: you take a statement of intent, strip it to its essential letters, and encode it into a symbol. Your conscious mind forgets the words — your deeper mind keeps executing the code. The Forge does the encoding. You do the charging. Reality handles the rest.

Let it run

◊ Questions ◊

Asked and answered

Do I need to believe in magic for this to work?

You need to believe in focus. A sigil is a focus technology — it compresses an intention so your attention can stop gripping it. Whether the mechanism is psychological, magical, or both is yours to decide. The practice works either way.

What happens after I pay?

Come back here and hit Member Login. Enter the same email you used at Whop checkout and we'll send you a magic link — no password to remember. Your access is already waiting.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from your Whop account, in about three clicks. Your grimoire stays saved — if you return, it's exactly where you left it.

Is my grimoire private?

Completely. Your sigils are stored under your account and readable by you alone. We don't look at your intentions. That's between you and the field.

Why do the same words always make the same sigil?

The Forge is deterministic — your name and intention are hashed into a seed that drives every line of the glyph. It's not random decoration; it's your words, encoded. Change one letter and the whole sigil changes.
